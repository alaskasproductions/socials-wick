Search for "best SMM panel" and you will find thousands of sites that look almost identical, with the same services, the same promises and suspiciously low prices. Some are excellent. Others disappear with your money or deliver engagement that vanishes in a week. Here are **nine things to check before you pay**, so you can pick a panel you can rely on for the long run.

## 1. Is there a real company behind it?

This is the fastest filter. A trustworthy panel shows:

- A **registered company name** and registration number
- A **physical business address**
- Clear **Terms & Conditions**, a **Privacy Policy** and a **Refund Policy**

If you cannot find who runs the website, you have no one to hold accountable. SocialsWick is operated by **TKOCY LTD**, registered in Cyprus in the EU. You can find our full company details on the [contact page](/contact).

## 2. How do they take payments?

Card payments through a licensed payment provider, with 3-D Secure, give you strong protection. Panels that accept **only** crypto or gift cards are harder to hold accountable if something goes wrong.

Check that the checkout is hosted by the payment provider, which means the panel never sees your card number.

## 3. Is there a clear refund policy?

Orders sometimes fail. For example, the target profile goes private or a service is paused. A good panel tells you in writing what happens then. Look for:

- Refunds for **undelivered or partially delivered** orders
- A way to get **unused balance** back
- A clear process for requesting a refund

Read ours here: [SocialsWick refund policy](/refund-policy).

## 4. Do they explain service quality honestly?

A panel that sells "1,000 real followers" for a few cents is not being honest. Good panels label quality clearly and explain the trade-offs between price, speed and retention. On SocialsWick every service carries a colour-coded tier, **Basic**, **Medium** or **Elite**, and the dashboard explains terms such as non-drop, drip-feed and refill. See [quality tiers explained](/blog/smm-service-quality-tiers-explained).

## 5. Do they ever ask for your password?

They should not. A legitimate panel only needs a public link or username. Any request for your login is an immediate red flag. More on this in [Is it safe to use an SMM panel?](/blog/is-it-safe-to-use-an-smm-panel).

## 6. How fast and responsive is support?

Send a question before you buy and see how fast you get an answer. A trustworthy panel offers several channels, such as email, a contact form and ideally live chat, and replies within hours, not weeks. Our [support team](/contact) is available 24/7.

## 7. Is the pricing transparent?

Prices should be shown **per 1,000** before you check out, with no hidden fees. A low minimum order is a good sign, because it lets you test a service cheaply before you commit. On SocialsWick orders start from **€1**. Learn how to [grow on a small budget](/blog/cheap-smm-panel-start-from-1-euro).

## 8. Can you track your orders?

You should see every order with its status (pending, in progress, completed, partial or cancelled) and the amount charged. Transparent tracking shows that the panel has nothing to hide.

## 9. Does it fit how you work?

- **Creators** need a simple checkout and good guidance.
- **Businesses** need stable, non-drop services and reliable support.
- **Agencies and resellers** need bulk ordering and an **API**. Read [SMM panel API for resellers](/blog/smm-panel-api-for-resellers).

## Why an EU-based panel can be a smart choice

If you are in Europe, an EU-registered panel brings extra protection. It must follow **GDPR** for your data and EU consumer rules for your purchases, and payments go through EU-regulated providers. That makes it much easier to resolve problems than with an anonymous site hosted anywhere in the world.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best SMM panel?

The best panel is the one that passes the checks above for your needs. That means real company details, secure payments, honest quality labels and support that answers. Price matters, but reliability matters more.

### Are cheap SMM panels a scam?

Not necessarily. Low prices can be genuine for budget services. The warning sign is cheap prices combined with no company information, no refund policy and unrealistic promises.

### How do I test a new panel?

Place a small order on a single post, check the delivery speed and retention over a week, and send support a question. Then decide whether to scale up.

### Do SMM panels offer refunds?

Reputable ones do for undelivered orders. Always read the refund policy before your first payment.
