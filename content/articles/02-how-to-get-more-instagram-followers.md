Instagram is more competitive than ever, but growing a following is still very possible if you combine good content with a smart strategy. Below are **11 practical ways to get more Instagram followers in 2026**. They cover everything from your bio to Reels, hashtags, timing and how to give a new account a safe head start.

## 1. Fix your profile before you chase followers

Your profile is your landing page. Before you invest time or money in growth, make sure a visitor understands in three seconds who you are and why they should follow.

- **Profile photo:** a clear face or logo that reads at small sizes.
- **Name field:** Instagram search indexes this field, so add a keyword, for example "Anna | Vegan Recipes".
- **Bio:** one line on what you post, one line on why it helps, then a call to action.
- **Link:** point to your best offer or a link page.
- **Highlights:** pin your best stories, such as FAQs, reviews and products.

## 2. Make Reels your main growth engine

Reels are still the format Instagram pushes hardest to non-followers. Focus on:

- A **hook in the first second**: a bold statement, a question or a visual surprise.
- **Short, re-watchable videos** of about 7 to 20 seconds for reach, longer for tutorials.
- **On-screen text**, because many people watch without sound.
- **Trending audio** used early, while it is still rising.

## 3. Post consistently, not constantly

Consistency beats volume. Three to five quality posts a week, plus daily stories, will outperform ten rushed posts. Pick a schedule you can keep for months, not just weeks.

## 4. Use hashtags and keywords the right way

Hashtags matter less than they used to, but keywords matter more. Instagram now reads your captions to understand what a post is about.

- Write captions with natural keywords, for example "easy high-protein breakfast ideas".
- Use **3 to 8 relevant hashtags** instead of 30 generic ones.
- Mix sizes: a few broad tags, several niche ones and one branded tag.

## 5. Post when your audience is online

Check **Insights → Total followers → Most active times**. Post 30 to 60 minutes before your peak so the first engagement arrives while your followers are scrolling.

## 6. Turn viewers into followers with carousels

Carousels get saved and shared, which are two of the strongest signals on Instagram. Educational carousels such as checklists, "5 mistakes" posts and before-and-after comparisons are especially effective. End every carousel with a slide that says why to follow you.

## 7. Engage like a real person

Spend 15 minutes a day replying to comments, answering DMs and leaving thoughtful comments on accounts in your niche. Accounts that start conversations get more reach than accounts that only broadcast.

## 8. Collaborate

Use **Collab posts** with creators of a similar size. The post appears on both profiles and both audiences see it. Giveaways with partner brands work well too, as long as the prize matches your niche.

## 9. Repurpose your best content

Your top-performing Reel is proof that an idea works. Remake it with a new hook, a different angle or updated information. You can also share it on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
