TikTok is still the fastest way for a new creator or brand to reach thousands of people. Unlike other platforms, a brand-new account can go viral with its first video. That only happens if you understand what makes TikTok push a video. Here are **12 proven ways to get more views on TikTok** in 2026.

## How the TikTok algorithm decides who sees your video

When you post, TikTok first shows your video to a small test audience. It then measures how people react:

- **Watch time and completion rate:** did they watch to the end, or even twice?
- **Rewatches:** loops are a strong signal.
- **Shares and saves:** these signal value.
- **Comments and likes.**
- **Follows from the video.**

If the test group reacts well, TikTok shows the video to a bigger group, and so on. Everything below is about winning those first rounds.

## 1. Hook viewers in the first second

You have about one second before people swipe away. Start with the most interesting moment, a bold claim ("Stop doing this with your skincare") or a visual that makes people ask "what is happening?". Never open with "Hi guys, today I…".

## 2. Keep videos as short as the idea allows

Shorter videos are easier to watch to the end, and completion rate is king. For entertainment, aim for 7 to 20 seconds. Tutorials and storytimes can run longer, but every second has to earn its place. Cut pauses, "umms" and slow intros.

## 3. Design for rewatches

Loops where the end flows into the start, fast text people want to pause and re-read, and a reveal at the very end all boost rewatches, and rewatches push completion above 100 percent.

## 4. Use trending sounds early

Check the **Creative Center** or the "trending" label in the sound library. Using a sound while it is still growing gives you a better chance than jumping on it when it has peaked.

## 5. Add on-screen text and captions

Many people watch with the sound off. Text on screen keeps them watching and helps TikTok understand the topic. Turn on auto-captions too.

## 6. Treat TikTok like a search engine (TikTok SEO)

More and more people search TikTok the way they search Google. Help your videos appear in search:

- Say your keyword out loud in the first seconds.
- Put it in the on-screen text and the caption.
- Use 3 to 5 specific hashtags rather than #fyp spam.

## 7. Post consistently at the right times

Check **Analytics → Followers → Follower activity** and post just before your audience's peak hours. One to three videos a day is a common rhythm for fast growth, but quality matters more than volume.

## 8. Reply to comments with videos

"Reply with video" turns one comment into new content. It also shows TikTok that your videos start conversations, and it is the easiest way to never run out of ideas.

## 9. Build series that make people follow

"Part 1, Part 2…" series give viewers a reason to follow so they do not miss the next part. Followers watch your new videos first, which helps each new upload pass the test round.

## 10. Stitch and duet with bigger creators

Stitches and duets place your content next to videos that already have momentum. Add real value, such as a reaction, a correction or a better tip, rather than just copying.

## 11. Go live when you can

Live streams build loyalty and bring people to your profile, where they discover your other videos. They are especially useful once you have regular viewers.
