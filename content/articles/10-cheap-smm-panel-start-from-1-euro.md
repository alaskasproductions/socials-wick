## Why start small?

Starting small is the smartest way to use any SMM panel, not just the cheapest one:

- **You test before you commit.** Every account and niche responds differently.
- **You learn which services hold up.** Drop rates and speeds vary between services.
- **Growth looks natural.** Small, steady boosts look more organic than one giant spike.
- **You risk almost nothing.** If a service is not right for you, you have lost a coffee, not a budget.

## What can a small budget get you?

Prices depend on the platform, the service and the quality tier, and they change as the catalog is updated. Check the live prices on the [services page](/services). As a general rule:

- **Views** (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) are the cheapest. A small budget can buy thousands.

A €1 to €5 budget is usually enough to boost a single post convincingly, or to test two or three different services side by side.

## How to spend a small budget smart

### 1. Boost your best post, not your worst

Engagement amplifies content that already works. Pick the post or video that is performing best organically and support it, rather than trying to rescue a post that nobody liked.

### 2. Start with views, then add signals

Views are cheap and give fast social proof. Once a video is moving, add a small number of **likes, shares or saves**. A mix of signals looks far more natural than views alone. Our [TikTok views guide](/blog/how-to-get-more-views-on-tiktok) explains why.

### 3. Time it right

Boost within the **first hour** after posting, while the platform is still deciding how widely to show your content.

### 4. Use the right tier for the job

- **Basic** for cheap, fast views and tests
- **Medium** for likes and followers you want to keep
- **Elite** when retention really matters

Read [Basic vs Medium vs Elite](/blog/smm-service-quality-tiers-explained) for the full comparison.

### 5. Top up your balance

If you order regularly, [create an account](/register) and add funds to your balance. Ordering from your balance is faster, because you do not enter card details every time, and you can track all your orders in one place.

## When "cheap" becomes expensive

Very low prices are not a problem in themselves. Views and budget likes are genuinely cheap to deliver. The warning signs are:

- **No company details and no refund policy.** If an order fails, there is nobody to ask.
- **Unrealistic promises**, such as "10,000 real, active followers for €1".
- **Requests for your password.** Never share it.
- **Payment only in crypto or gift cards.**

A cheap panel you can trust gives you clear prices, honest quality labels and refunds for undelivered orders. Our checklist [How to choose a trustworthy SMM panel](/blog/how-to-choose-a-trustworthy-smm-panel) covers the rest.
