Ordering on SocialsWick takes about a minute. You do not even need an account for your first order. This guide walks through **both ways to order**: the quick checkout on our homepage, and the dashboard for returning customers who keep a balance. If you are new to SMM panels, start with [What is an SMM panel?](/blog/what-is-an-smm-panel).

## Before you start: have the right link ready

The most common reason an order is delayed is a wrong link, so it helps to know which one you need:

| You are ordering | Link to use |
|---|---|
| Followers or subscribers | Your **profile or channel** link, or your username |
| Likes, views, saves, shares or comments | The link to the **specific post, Reel or video** |
| Telegram members | Your **channel or group** link |

Two more things:

- **Make your account public** while the order is running. Private profiles cannot receive delivery.
- **Do not change your username** until the order is complete.

Our order form checks the link format and warns you if, for example, you paste a profile link for a TikTok views order.

## Option 1: Quick checkout, no account needed

This is the fastest way to place a first order.

### Step 1: Pick a service

On the [homepage](/) or the [services page](/services), choose your platform, for example Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, then the type of service, such as followers, likes or views.

Next, choose the **quality tier**:

- 🟡 **Basic:** the lowest price, ideal for testing.
- 🟢 **Medium:** a balance of price and retention.
- 🔵 **Elite:** premium, non-drop and organic-grade.

Not sure which to pick? Read [Basic vs Medium vs Elite](/blog/smm-service-quality-tiers-explained).

Then choose a quantity package. The price updates instantly, and orders start from just **€1**.

### Step 2: Paste your link

Enter your profile link or username, or the post or video link, depending on the service. The form tells you which one it needs.

### Step 3: Enter your email and pay

Enter your email address so we can send your order confirmation, then pay securely by card on the payment page. Card payments are handled by a licensed payment provider with 3-D Secure, and SocialsWick never sees or stores your card number.

That is it. As soon as the payment is confirmed, your order is placed automatically and delivery begins, usually within minutes.

We also create an account for you in the background and email you a link to set your password. You can then log in any time to follow your order.

## Option 2: Order from your dashboard

If you order regularly, your dashboard is faster, because you pay from your balance without entering card details each time.

### 1. Add funds

Go to **Dashboard → Add Funds**, enter an amount and pay by card. The balance is credited as soon as the payment is confirmed.

### 2. Place a new order

Open **Dashboard → New Order**. You can:

- Browse categories by platform and filter by tier, such as instant start, non-drop or drip-feed.
- Open **See Details** on any category for a plain-language explanation of each service type.
- Paste your link, enter the quantity and confirm. The price is charged to your balance.

### 3. Track progress

**Dashboard → Orders** shows every order with its status:

- **Pending / In progress:** delivery has started or is queued.
- **Completed:** the full quantity has been delivered.
- **Partial:** only part could be delivered, and the rest is refunded to your balance.
- **Cancelled:** nothing could be delivered, and the full amount is refunded to your balance.

## Tips for the best results

- **Start small** with a new service to see how it performs on your account.
- **Use drip-feed** for large quantities so growth looks natural.
- **Boost your strongest content.** A post that already performs well benefits most. See our tips for [Instagram](/blog/how-to-get-more-instagram-followers), [TikTok](/blog/how-to-get-more-views-on-tiktok) and [YouTube](/blog/how-to-grow-a-youtube-channel).
- **Keep your account secure.** Turn on two-factor authentication under Account settings.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account to order?

No. You can order as a guest with just your email. We create an account for you automatically so you can track the order.

### How do I know my order has started?

You receive a confirmation email, and the order appears under **Orders** in your dashboard with its live status.

### What if I entered the wrong link?

Contact [our support team](/contact) as soon as possible with your order ID. If delivery has not started yet, we can usually help.

### Can I get a refund?

Undelivered orders, or the undelivered part of an order, are refunded to your balance. Unused balance can be refunded to your card within 14 days. Read the full [refund policy](/refund-policy).
