If you manage social media for clients or run your own SMM panel, placing orders by hand quickly becomes a full-time job. An **SMM panel API** solves that. It lets your software place and track orders automatically. This guide explains how an SMM API works, what you can automate, how resellers build a business on top of it and how to talk to us about API access at SocialsWick.

## What is an SMM panel API?

An API (application programming interface) is a way for two systems to talk to each other. With an SMM panel API, **your** website, app or script sends a request to the panel, for example "deliver 1,000 views to this video", and receives an order ID back. You can then ask for the status of that order at any time.

Everything you can do by hand in the dashboard, your software can do automatically, 24 hours a day.

## What can you automate?

Typical API actions include:

- **List services:** fetch the current catalog with names, categories, rates per 1,000 and minimum and maximum quantities.
- **Place orders:** submit a service ID, a link and a quantity.
- **Check order status:** see progress, start count and remaining quantity.
- **Check your balance:** so your system knows when to top up.

## Who uses an SMM API?

### Social media agencies

Agencies bundle engagement into their client packages, for example a launch boost for every new campaign. With the API, the boost is ordered automatically when the campaign goes live.

### SMM resellers and panel owners

Many SMM panels source their services from bigger wholesale panels. A reseller connects a supplier's API to their own storefront, sets their own prices and serves their own customers. The supplier's catalog becomes their catalog.

### Developers and growth tools

Scheduling tools, creator apps and marketing platforms add "boost this post" features to their products through an API.

## How to start an SMM reseller business

1. **Choose a niche.** For example, TikTok creators in your country, local businesses or musicians on Spotify.
2. **Pick a reliable supplier.** Look for stable services, clear quality labels, fast support and an API. Our checklist [How to choose a trustworthy SMM panel](/blog/how-to-choose-a-trustworthy-smm-panel) covers what to look for.
3. **Set up your storefront.** Use ready-made SMM panel software or build your own site, then connect it to the supplier's API.
4. **Price for margin.** Add your markup to the supplier's rate per 1,000. Keep an eye on popular services, where competition is highest.
5. **Offer great support.** Your customers will judge you on how quickly you answer and resolve issues.
6. **Market your panel** with content, social media and SEO, just like this blog.

## What to look for in a supplier API

| Feature | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Stable service IDs | Your storefront does not break when the catalog changes |
| Clear quality labels | You can describe services honestly to your customers |
| Accurate order status | You can pass live status to your customers |
| Refunds for failed orders | Partial and cancelled orders are credited back automatically |
| Responsive support | Problems get solved before your customers notice |
| Secure payments | You can top up your balance safely |

## Best practices for API resellers

- **Sync the catalog regularly** so prices and availability stay accurate.
- **Validate links before submitting.** Profile-level services such as followers use a username or profile link. Post-level services such as likes, views and comments need a post or video link.
- **Poll status sensibly.** Check running orders every few minutes, not every second.
- **Handle partial orders.** Credit your customers for undelivered quantities.
- **Keep your API key secret.** Store it on your server, never in browser code.
