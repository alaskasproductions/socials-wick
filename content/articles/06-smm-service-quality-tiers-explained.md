Two services can both say "1,000 Instagram followers" and still be completely different products. One might cost a few cents and slowly drop over time, while another costs more and stays put. To make this easy, every service on SocialsWick carries a **colour-coded quality tier**. This guide explains what each tier means, what "drop", "refill" and "non-drop" really mean, and how to choose the right option for your goal.

## Why SMM service quality varies

Engagement comes from different sources. Some services use automated, high-volume networks. Others use aged accounts with history, and the best ones rely on real, organic users. The source affects:

- **Price:** more realistic sources cost more to produce.
- **Retention:** how many followers or likes stay over time.
- **Speed:** how quickly an order can be delivered.
- **Appearance:** whether profiles look active and real.

## 🟡 Basic: best price, high volume

**Basic** is the most affordable tier, with the lowest prices in our catalog and automated delivery.

**Good for:**

- Testing a service before a bigger order
- Budget campaigns where the number matters more than longevity
- Views on content where a fast push is the goal

**Keep in mind:** counts can fall back over time and delivery speed varies. Basic services usually do not include a refill guarantee.

## 🟢 Medium: balanced and reliable

**Medium** is the middle ground. These services are checked for retention and behave far more naturally than Basic ones, so your growth looks steady rather than sudden. Selected Medium services include a **refill period**.

**Good for:**

- Everyday growth on personal and business profiles
- Creators who want value without big drops
- Supporting launches and campaigns

## 🔵 Elite: premium, non-drop and organic-grade

**Elite** is our top tier. Engagement comes from organic, real-user methods, so results are the most stable in the catalog, with non-drop retention. Elite services often bring additional genuine interaction to your content.

**Good for:**

- Brands, agencies and public figures who cannot afford drops
- Accounts where credibility matters more than price
- Long-term growth you want to keep

## Key terms explained

### Drop and drop rate

A **drop** is when some delivered followers or likes disappear over time, often because the platform removes inactive accounts. The **drop rate** is the percentage that disappears.

### Refill

A **refill guarantee** means that if the count drops within a set period, for example 30 days, the service tops it back up. Check the service description for the exact refill period.

### Non-drop

**Non-drop** services are built to keep what they deliver, even through platform updates. They cost more, but you do not need to worry about losing numbers.

### Drip-feed and gradual delivery

With **drip-feed**, your order is released in smaller portions over time instead of all at once. The result looks more organic. We recommend it for any large order.

### Instant start and high retention

**Instant start** services begin within minutes. **High retention** view services keep viewers watching for longer, which matters for YouTube watch time.

## How to choose the right tier

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **How long do I need the result to last?** For a one-off push, Basic is fine. If you want to keep the numbers, choose Medium or Elite.
2. **How visible is my account?** Brands and public profiles should choose Elite or non-drop, because a visible drop looks worse than slower growth.
3. **What is my budget?** Start with Basic or Medium to test, then scale up with the tier that performed best.

A good strategy for most creators is to use **Basic for views** on new posts, **Medium for likes and followers**, and **Elite for a profile's core follower base**.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Elite followers real people?

Elite services are sourced through organic, real-user methods, which is why they are the most stable. As with any service, results can vary by platform and account.

### Why does Basic cost so little?

Basic uses automated high-volume sources. That keeps prices very low but means retention is not guaranteed.

### Can I mix tiers on one account?

Yes, and many customers do. They use Basic for views, for example, and Elite for followers.

### What if my followers drop?

If your service has a refill period, it will be topped up within that time. For peace of mind, choose non-drop or Elite services.
